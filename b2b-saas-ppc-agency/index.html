Why Search Is The Highest-Intent Channel For SaaS

Every other channel interrupts. Search captures people who are already typing "best [category] software" or your competitor's name into Google — the highest-intent moment in the entire buying journey. For B2B SaaS, that makes Google Ads the channel where poor execution is most expensive and good execution compounds fastest.

Most agencies running B2B SaaS Search accounts stop at cost-per-click and cost-per-lead. Those metrics are meaningless without knowing which keywords and campaigns actually produce paying customers. A $40 CPC on a competitor term can be far cheaper than a $12 CPC on a generic category term if the competitor traffic converts to paid customers at 3x the rate.

FRX Media builds B2B SaaS PPC accounts around your CAC target and sales motion — category Search for demand capture, competitor conquesting for differentiated products, and Performance Max for incremental reach — all tracked through to paid customer value.

FAQ

Questions About Our B2B SaaS PPC Agency

How much should a B2B SaaS company spend on Google Ads?+

It depends on category competitiveness and deal value, but most B2B SaaS companies need at least $3,000-$5,000/month to gather enough Search data to optimize meaningfully, especially in competitive categories where CPCs can run $10-50+ per click. Niche categories with less competition can start smaller.

Is it worth bidding on competitor brand terms for SaaS?+

Competitor conquesting campaigns can work well for SaaS, particularly when your product has a clear differentiator against the competitor (price, feature, ease of use). They typically have lower volume than category terms but very high intent. We test them as a supplementary campaign, not the core of a Search strategy.

Does Performance Max work for B2B SaaS lead generation?+

Performance Max can work for B2B SaaS but requires careful setup — audience signals, negative keyword lists via search themes, and conversion tracking tied to qualified leads rather than all form fills. Used correctly it captures incremental demand outside your Search campaigns; used carelessly it wastes budget on low-intent traffic.

How do you measure Google Ads success for SaaS beyond clicks?+

We track the funnel through to trial-to-paid conversion or demo-to-close rate using your product analytics or CRM data, and report on CAC and pipeline value by campaign and keyword group — not just click volume or cost-per-click.

How long does it take to see results from B2B SaaS Google Ads?+

Search campaigns typically start generating qualified traffic within days since they're capturing existing demand, but meaningful CAC optimization usually takes 6-8 weeks of data. B2B SaaS sales cycles can extend this further before you see full pipeline-to-close attribution.
