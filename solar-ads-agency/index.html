Solar Ads For Installation Companies

Why Solar Ads Need Real Qualification, Not Just Volume

Solar is one of the most qualification-heavy home-services categories on Meta and Google — a "lead" that doesn't own their home, doesn't have roof space, or can't qualify for financing is worse than no lead at all. Agencies that chase cheap cost-per-lead without qualification criteria hand sales teams a pile of unusable contacts.

Solar demand also spikes hard around incentive changes — federal tax credit deadlines, state rebate announcements, and net metering policy shifts create urgency windows that generic, flat-spend campaigns miss entirely.

FRX Media builds solar lead-gen campaigns around real qualification criteria — homeownership, roof condition, average electric bill, credit tier — so your sales team spends time on homeowners who can actually convert to installs.

FAQ

Questions About Our Solar Ads Agency

How much does Meta Ads lead generation cost for solar companies?+

Solar lead costs vary heavily by market, incentive availability, and homeowner qualification criteria (roof type, credit tier, homeownership), but a well-run Meta lead-gen campaign for solar typically lands in the $15-40 cost-per-lead range for pre-qualified homeowner leads. We optimize toward your specific market's cost-per-lead once we have a few weeks of data.

Do you qualify leads for solar eligibility before they reach my sales team?+

Yes. We build lead forms with qualification questions — homeownership status, roof condition, average electric bill, credit tier — so your sales team spends time on homeowners who can actually qualify for financing or purchase, not just anyone who clicked.

How do solar incentive changes affect ad campaigns?+

Federal and state incentive changes (like tax credit step-downs or net metering policy shifts) create urgency spikes in demand. We monitor policy changes in your market and adjust messaging and budget pacing to capture the resulting surge in homeowner interest.

Do you run Google Ads alongside Meta for solar lead generation?+

Yes. FRX Media runs Meta Ads, Google Ads (including Local Services Ads where available), and Microsoft Ads for solar companies, and we recommend the channel mix based on where your best-qualified leads actually convert to installs.

How fast can I see results from a solar ads campaign?+

Most solar lead-gen campaigns start generating leads within the first 1-2 weeks once targeting and creative are validated, with cost-per-qualified-lead typically stabilizing by week 3-4 as we refine the qualification criteria in the lead form.
